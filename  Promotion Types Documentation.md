# 🎯 Promotion Types Documentation

## Introduction

Promotions are tools that stores use to attract customers, boost sales, and increase loyalty. Each store can have multiple promotions running simultaneously, and each promotion has its own type, conditions, and parameters.

This document introduces 10 main types of promotions, along with their use cases and examples.

---

## 1. **Coupons / Promo Codes**

**Description:**
Customers can enter a discount code to reduce their purchase by a specific percentage or amount.

**Use Cases:**

* Attract new customers or encourage immediate purchases
* Create a sense of exclusivity by offering unique codes

**Examples:**

* “10% off with code OFF10”
* “50,000 Toman off your first purchase with code WELCOME50”

---

## 2. **Cashback**

**Description:**
A percentage of the purchase amount is returned to the customer’s wallet or account after purchase.

**Use Cases:**

* Encourage repeat purchases
* Increase customer loyalty by providing added value

**Examples:**

* “5% of your purchase amount will be credited back to your wallet”
* “Spend 200,000 Toman and receive 10,000 Toman credit”

---

## 3. **Referral Discount**

**Description:**
Customers and their referred friends both receive discounts when a referral is made.

**Use Cases:**

* Acquire new customers without direct advertising costs
* Encourage existing users to promote the store

**Examples:**

* “Invite a friend and get 50,000 Toman credit”
* “Your friends get 10% off using your referral code”

---

## 4. **Conditional / Tiered Discount**

**Description:**
Discounts are applied when a purchase reaches a specified threshold.

**Use Cases:**

* Increase average order value
* Encourage larger purchases in a single order

**Examples:**

* “10% off for orders above 500,000 Toman”
* “15% off for orders over 1,000,000 Toman”

---

## 5. **Percentage / Fixed Discount**

**Description:**
Reduces the price either by a percentage or a fixed amount.

**Use Cases:**

* Provide a simple and general discount
* Suitable for all customers

**Examples:**

* “20% off all products”
* “100,000 Toman off purchases above 500,000 Toman”

---

## 6. **Flash Sale / Limited Time Discount**

**Description:**
Discounts are available for a short, limited time.

**Use Cases:**

* Encourage quick purchases
* Create urgency and boost immediate sales

**Examples:**

* “30% off only until tonight”
* “Black Friday: 50% off all products”

---

## 7. **Free Shipping Incentive**

**Description:**
Instead of a cash discount, free shipping is offered.

**Use Cases:**

* Reduce the perception of extra costs for customers
* Increase online purchase rates

**Examples:**

* “Free shipping for orders over 300,000 Toman”
* “Free shipping for VIP members”

---

## 8. **Loyalty Points / Reward Points**

**Description:**
Customers earn points for purchases, which can later be redeemed for discounts or gifts.

**Use Cases:**

* Increase repeat purchases and customer loyalty
* Encourage users to return to the store

**Examples:**

* “Earn 1 point for every 10,000 Toman spent”
* “Redeem 50 points for a gift”

---

## 9. **Behavioral / Event-based Discounts**

**Description:**
Discounts are applied based on user behavior or special events.

**Use Cases:**

* Increase customer engagement
* Provide personalized or event-specific discounts

**Examples:**

* “10% off on your birthday”
* “50,000 Toman credit for the first app login each month”

---

## 10. **Stackable / Multi-step Discounts**

**Description:**
Multiple promotions can be applied to a single order, or discounts can be activated in steps.

**Use Cases:**

* Increase flexibility in discount systems
* Encourage larger purchases and simultaneous use of multiple promotions

**Examples:**

* “10% off + free shipping on one order”
* “Orders above 500,000 Toman = 10% off + 50 loyalty points”

---

### 🔹 Summary

These 10 types of promotions cover all common store scenarios and can be combined or customized for each store.
With this categorization, a comprehensive loyalty and promotion system can be designed for any store.
